# Workspace members and roles

Acme Boards is a fictional product used in Caliban's demos.

## Invite a member

Open **Settings > Members** and choose **Invite**. Enter one or more e-mail addresses and pick a
role. Invitations expire after 7 days. Each member uses one seat of the subscription.

## Roles

There are three roles: **Admin** (billing, security, members), **Editor** (create and change
boards and reports) and **Viewer** (read only). Change a role from the member's row in
**Settings > Members**.
