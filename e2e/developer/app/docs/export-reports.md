# Exporting reports

Acme Boards is a fictional product used in Caliban's demos.

## Export a report

Open the report, then choose **Export** in the top right corner. Pick **CSV** for spreadsheets or
**PDF** for sharing. A CSV export holds at most 10,000 rows; for a larger report, filter it by date
range first and export each range.

## Schedule a weekly export

In the report, choose **Export > Schedule**, pick the day and the format, and add the recipients.
Scheduled exports are sent every week at 07:00 in the workspace's time zone. Only workspace admins
and editors can schedule exports.
