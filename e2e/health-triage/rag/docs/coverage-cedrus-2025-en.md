# Cedrus Mutual Essential plan: coverage policy (2025 edition)

Policy CM-ESS, 2025 edition, replaced on 1 January 2026. Cedrus Mutual is a fictional insurer, and
this fictional policy was written for the Caliban end-to-end test.

## Outpatient consultations

Consultations with a general practitioner or a specialist are covered at 70% at partner
facilities.

## Imaging

| Examination | Covered | Pre-authorisation |
|---|---|---|
| Echocardiogram | 50% | Not required |
| Knee MRI | 40% | Not required |
| X-ray | 70% | Not required |

## Emergency care

Emergency department visits are covered at 90% after a deductible of 80 USD per visit.
