# Cedrus Mutual Essential plan: coverage policy

Policy CM-ESS, in force from 1 January 2026. Cedrus Mutual is a fictional insurer, and this
fictional policy was written for the Caliban end-to-end test.

## Outpatient consultations

Consultations with a general practitioner or a specialist (cardiology, pediatrics, dermatology,
orthopedics) are covered at 80% at partner facilities. The member pays the remaining 20% at the
facility.

## Imaging

| Examination | Covered | Pre-authorisation |
|---|---|---|
| Echocardiogram | 70% | Required |
| Knee MRI | 60% | Required |
| X-ray | 80% | Not required |

The Essential plan covers a knee MRI at 60% after pre-authorisation. An MRI is covered only when an
orthopedic surgeon or a sports medicine physician prescribes it after a consultation. A knee MRI
without pre-authorisation is not covered.

## Pre-authorisation

The facility asks Cedrus Mutual for pre-authorisation on the member's behalf. Cedrus Mutual
answers within 48 hours on working days. An urgent examination ordered by an emergency department
needs no pre-authorisation.

## Emergency care

Emergency department visits are covered at 100% after a deductible of 50 USD per visit, in any
facility, partner or not.

## Exclusions

Cosmetic procedures, examinations without a prescription and care received abroad without prior
agreement are not covered.
