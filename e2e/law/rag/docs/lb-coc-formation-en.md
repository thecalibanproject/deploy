# Lebanon, Code of Obligations and Contracts: how a contract is formed

> Demo excerpt. A paraphrased, simplified summary of the principles of the Lebanese Code of Obligations and Contracts (COC, 1932) on contract formation, written for a demo. Article numbers are not reproduced: check the official text before relying on it.

## Consent is enough

The COC, largely modelled on French civil law, provides that a contract is formed by the agreement of the parties: an offer and an acceptance that meet on the essential terms. Unless the law requires a form, no particular form is needed.

## Validity

A valid contract needs consent free of defects (mistake, fraud, duress, and lesion where the law provides for it), parties with capacity, a possible and lawful object, and a lawful cause.

## Silence and contracts at a distance

As a rule, silence is not acceptance. It can be when the offer concerns business relations the parties already have. Between parties who are not in the same place, the COC sets when and where the contract is formed: check the applicable rule for the case at hand.

## Firm practice

For contracts that touch both Lebanon and France, identify the governing law when the matter is opened; the Beirut office signs off any analysis of Lebanese law.
