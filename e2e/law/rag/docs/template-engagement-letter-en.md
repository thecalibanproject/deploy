# Template: engagement letter (Beirut and Paris offices)

> Demo document. A fictional template of Hadad & Associés, written for a demo.

## Structure

1. Parties: the client (legal name, registered address) and the firm's office that will act.
2. Scope of the engagement: the matter, what is included and what is not.
3. Conflict check: the letter states that a conflict check was run on the date of the letter, with its result.
4. Team: the responsible lawyer and the people who will work on the matter.
5. Fees: hourly rates (USD for Beirut, EUR for Paris), a fixed fee, or a cap; disbursements; invoicing every month.
6. Governing law and disputes: Lebanese law for the Beirut office, French law for the Paris office.
7. Confidentiality and personal data: the firm processes the client's personal data only for the engagement.

## Before sending

The responsible lawyer checks the conflict check result and the fee arrangement recorded in the practice database.
