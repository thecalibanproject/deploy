# Firm policy: conflict-of-interest checks

> Demo document. A fictional policy of Hadad & Associés (Beirut and Paris), written for a demo.

## When

A conflict check is run before the firm accepts a new client or a new matter, and again when a new party joins an existing matter.

## How

1. Search every party of every matter, open, on hold or closed, in both offices: clients, adverse parties, counterparties and third parties.
2. A direct conflict exists when the prospective client's opponent is a current client of the firm, or when the prospective client is an adverse party in a matter the firm handles.
3. A possible conflict exists when a name is similar, or when a party is related to a current client (same group of companies, same family).
4. Record the result in the matter-opening form.

## Decision

A direct conflict means the firm declines, unless every client concerned gives informed written consent and the managing partner approves. A possible conflict goes to the managing partner, who decides within two working days.
